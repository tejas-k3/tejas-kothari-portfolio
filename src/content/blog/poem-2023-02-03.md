In shadows cast by clouds of gray,  
I find myself in disarray,  
A soul immersed in depths depressed,  
Longing for solace, seeking rest  
A trusted beacon, now withdrawn,  
The one I thought I could rely on,  
But in my struggle, cold and grim,  
I fight my demons, all alone within  
An office poisoned by toxicity's sting,  
Where hopes and dreams no longer sing,  
In solitude's embrace, I yearn for a touch,  
Yet my voice grows silent, hesitant to reach out much  
Yet through the darkness, I endure,  
For within my heart, resilience pure
