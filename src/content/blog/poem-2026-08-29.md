9 years ago we shared a tiny room  
Didn't know we'd live through this much chaos and doom  
Roommates first, then brothers for life  
Best thing I got out of college, no lie  
You're smart as hell, and I fukin love that it’s so true  
You pick one goal and somehow break right through  
Whatever you chase, you'll probably win  
Annoying talent should honestly be a sin  
But listen up, cunt, here's my advice  
Go sleep more hours for once in your life  
And eat some actual food, on time, all times  
Your body deserves better, also this line rhymes  
I'm proud as fuk of the man you became  
You've earned every bit of success in your name  
I'll clap the loudest when you reach your peak  
Even if I roast your black ass every week  
Now let me say what pisses me off too  
You forget to call or text me fuk you  
One msg won't make your fingers fall  
So answer your phone once in a while, that's all  
I'm still grinding, chasing my own dream  
Building a life bigger than it may seem  
One day I'll pull up with a Lambo key  
Throw it at you and say, “This one's free”  
Then we'll disappear on some random ride  
Windows down with zero plans to guide  
Collecting stories instead of gold  
Still acting 20 when we're 80 years old  
I fucking swear, and this part stays true  
There's nobody's company I value like you  
Happy bday, brother, now here's my wish  
Stay happy, stay healthy... and text me, bich
