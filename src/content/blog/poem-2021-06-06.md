I write this with deep sorrow in my heart  
I feel like being in store full of things with an empty kart  
Why this love is so painful and inspiring enough to put textual art?  
This immense emotion is too heavy to control or discard  
Why you leave me with no explicit return time, you already had headstart  
Your vanished so sudden leaving me too stun to read or to think smart  
I feel like a target covered by numerous darts  
O double paneer roll I miss you so hard!  
O Pizza I hope I get to taste you so that my heart can stop being apart!  
O Manchurian and pasta, let's fix a date and get Swiggy to print it on a card  
All the tasty food I miss... Let's hope soon I get them at my day's start
