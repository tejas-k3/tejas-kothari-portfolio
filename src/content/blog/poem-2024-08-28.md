I saw a family of 4, looking at train  
They were standing on walkway of bridge without worrying about rain  
I saw a family of 4, looking at train  
Reminded me time when one of my toys was a crane  
I saw a family of 4, looking at train  
Nostalgia hit me as everyone and I use to sit at platform and wait  
I saw a family of 4, looking at train  
It reminded me of family of 4, who used to look at train
